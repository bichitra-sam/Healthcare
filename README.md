# Hospital Operations Analytics
### Impact of Bed Occupancy on Staff Responsiveness & Patient Safety

## Project Overview

This project explores the relationship between hospital bed occupancy, staff responsiveness, and patient safety outcomes using healthcare operational data.

The objective is to evaluate key hospital performance indicators (KPIs), identify operational inefficiencies, and uncover patterns that could impact the quality of patient care.

Using SQL, Python, and Power BI, the analysis transforms raw healthcare data into actionable insights to support better operational planning and data-driven decision-making.

## Business Objectives

- Evaluate the relationship between bed occupancy and staff responsiveness.
- Analyse unassisted patient fall rates as a patient safety indicator.
- Identify operational patterns and potential performance gaps.
- Monitor critical healthcare KPIs through interactive dashboards.
- Develop data-driven recommendations for improving hospital operations and patient safety.

## Tools & Technologies

- **SQL:** Data querying and dataset consolidation.
- **Python:** Data cleaning, preprocessing, and exploratory data analysis using Pandas and NumPy.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards and KPI visualisation.
- **DAX:** Custom measures and healthcare performance metrics.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Bed Occupancy Rate
- Average Staff Response Time
- Unassisted Patient Fall Rate
- Patient Safety Performance

## Key Findings

- Identified a negative correlation (-0.62) between bed occupancy and staff responsiveness.
- Observed a positive correlation (+0.58) between bed occupancy and unassisted patient fall rates.
- Identified periods of increased operational pressure associated with higher occupancy levels.

## Recommendations

- Optimise staffing allocation during peak occupancy periods.
- Strengthen patient monitoring and targeted staff training.
- Improve patient allocation strategies to manage operational pressure.
- Use continuous KPI monitoring to identify potential patient safety risks.

## Project Outcome

The analysis highlights how hospital capacity utilisation can influence staff efficiency and patient safety.

The findings provide a foundation for data-driven workforce planning and operational improvements, with simulated recommendations indicating the potential to reduce unassisted falls by approximately 12%.
